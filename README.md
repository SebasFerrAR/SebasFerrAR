# Sebastián Ferreyra

CEO & Creative Director at [DigitalArts®](https://bydigitalarts.com) and co-founder of [Arenma®](https://arenma.io). Design, technology and customer experience, from Buenos Aires, Argentina.

I started at Shell Argentina, first at a service station and then as a visual designer, and went on to creative leadership roles with US companies. That path left me with the idea behind my work: people make the difference.

Today I lead a team that builds websites, apps, AI-powered software and campaigns for clients in Argentina, the US, Canada, the UK and Uruguay.

## What I build

- **[DigitalArts®](https://bydigitalarts.com)**: design and software company from Argentina (D.A. TECNOLOGÍAS VISUALES S.R.L.). Websites, apps, AI-powered software, branding, motion, 3D and paid media.
- **[Arenma®](https://arenma.io)**: AI-powered operations platform for teams. Projects, requests, clients and conversations in one place, with passwordless sign-in. Web, iOS, Android, Windows and macOS.
